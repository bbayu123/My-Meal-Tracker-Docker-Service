# Contributing & Support

Thank you for your interest in this project!

## 🚫 Contributions and Support are Closed

This repository is built strictly for my own **private purposes and personal workflows**. 

* **Works For Me:** If the code works for my specific setup, it is considered complete. 
* **No Bug Reports or Issues:** Please do not open issues or reach out regarding bugs, edge cases, or broken features. Unsolicited issues will be closed without a response.
* **No Pull Requests:** Pull requests, optimizations, or feature additions will not be reviewed or merged.

## 💡 Use it as Inspiration

You are fully welcome to fork this repository, copy the code, or use it as inspiration for your own projects (commercial or open-source) under the terms of the `LICENSE.md`. 

If you need to fix a bug or add a feature, please **fork the repository** and maintain your own version. Thank you for respecting the private scope of this project!
